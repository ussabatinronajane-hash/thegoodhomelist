## Notes

- The photos are decorative category art. They do not show products reviewed on the
  site, and the site does not claim they do.
- The Gladkov, Sergi and Halmshaw photos were cropped from larger originals. The
  original files are not stored here.
- The Smart Home photo shows a Google Home Mini. It is used as a generic example of a smart speaker. The site does not endorse or review it, and the tile label stays neutral.
